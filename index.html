        Two ways to watch together

        New

        In a CineTab room

        No meeting needed. Friends join from their browser with a link, a QR code, or the room code and password.

        1. Create a room and open a movie. Pick it from your computer or drag it onto the page.
        2. Invite friends. Send the link or show the QR code, and let them in as they arrive.
        3. Press play. Everyone watches your movie in sync, or their own copy of it, and chats beside the video.

        On Google Meet, Zoom or Teams

        Already on a call? Share the CineTab tab with its sound.

        1. Choose a movie. Pick a video from your computer or drag it onto the page. It opens instantly.
        2. Share this tab. In your meeting choose Share screen → Chrome Tab, select CineTab and switch on Also share tab audio.
        3. Press play. Everyone sees and hears the movie. Go fullscreen with F and turn on subtitles with C.

        Questions

        How do I share a video with sound on Google Meet?

        Open your video in CineTab in Chrome or Edge. In Google Meet click Share screen, choose Chrome Tab (or A tab), pick the CineTab tab and switch on Also share tab audio, then press play. Sharing a tab sends smooth video and the movie's sound; sharing a window or your whole screen doesn't send tab audio.

        Can I watch a movie together on Zoom or Microsoft Teams?

        Yes. In Zoom click Share, pick the browser tab with CineTab and tick Share sound. In Teams choose Share, select the CineTab tab or browser window and turn on Include sound. Everyone on the call watches and hears the movie playing on your computer.

        Can I play a video from my computer in a browser tab?

        That's exactly what CineTab does. Pick or drag in any video or music file and it plays right in the tab: MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV, TS, MP3, FLAC and more, with subtitles, playback speed and fullscreen. The file stays on your computer.

        How do I watch together without a video call?

        Use a CineTab room. Click Watch together → Create a room, open a movie and invite friends with the link, the QR code, or the room code and password. You let each person in, then everyone watches your movie streamed from your browser, perfectly in step with you, and can chat beside the video. A friend who has the same movie can play their own copy instead; CineTab keeps it in sync with yours.

        Are CineTab rooms private and secure?

        Yes. Browsers in a room connect directly to the host's browser (peer-to-peer) and WebRTC encrypts the video, sound and chat end to end. To find each other, browsers swap connection details through a public relay, encrypted with a key made from the room code and password, so the relay can't read them and never sees the password. Nothing is stored anywhere: when the room ends, the chat is gone. You let each person in, can remove anyone and can lock the room. As on any video call, the host and guests can see each other's IP address; guests can't see other guests' addresses.

        How many people can join a room?

        Up to 12 guests. If you stream your movie, your internet upload is shared between everyone watching the stream, about 2.5 Mbit/s each at standard quality, so 3 to 5 viewers is comfortable on most home connections. Guests who play their own copy of the movie use almost no bandwidth. You can lower the stream quality in the room settings.

        A friend can't connect to my room.

        Check that they typed the code and password exactly and that your CineTab tab is still open. Then use Check my network in the join dialog: it shows what the network allows. Some networks, such as campus, office or hotel Wi-Fi and certain mobile data plans, block direct connections between browsers or keep devices on the same Wi-Fi apart; switching one of you to a different network (for example a phone hotspot) usually fixes it. Streaming needs Chrome or Edge on the host's computer; guests can use Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

        Do my friends need to install anything?

        No. On a call, your friends just join the meeting and watch your shared tab. In a CineTab room, they open your invite link in their browser, on a computer or a phone.

        Is my movie uploaded anywhere?

        Never to a server. CineTab is a static web page — the browser reads the file from your disk and plays it locally. In a CineTab room, the video is streamed directly from your browser to your friends' browsers, encrypted, and isn't saved on their side. Closing the tab leaves no copy behind.

        My friends can see the movie but can't hear it.

        Stop sharing and share again, choosing the Chrome Tab option and turning on Also share tab audio. Sharing a window or the entire screen doesn't send tab audio. Tab audio works in Chrome and Edge.

        Which video and audio formats work?

        Almost all of them. MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM and music files like MP3, M4A, FLAC and WAV play instantly. AVI, WMV, FLV, TS and movies with DTS or Dolby (AC3) sound are converted automatically on your computer the first time — usually a few minutes for a movie. Old AVI/WMV video that has to be fully re-encoded takes longer.

        Where does the conversion happen?

        Right in your browser. The first time, CineTab downloads its converter (about 31 MB, ffmpeg built for the web). Your movie is never uploaded. The converted copies of your three most recent files are kept in this browser's storage so they open instantly next time. They're listed near the top of this page, where you can play or delete them.

        Can I use CineTab without this website or without internet?

        Yes. Download the player as a single HTML file, save it anywhere and double-click it to open it in your browser. It's just the player, without this home page, and everything it needs (icons, fonts, scripts) is inside that one file, so it works with no internet connection. Two differences from the website: the converter for formats like AVI or DTS sound needs internet the first time it's used (to download ffmpeg), and because browsers don't allow file storage for pages opened from disk, converted copies aren't saved between visits and very large movies may not fit in memory while converting.

        Can I watch YouTube videos together?

        Yes. Search YouTube from the dashboard (or paste a video or playlist link) and it plays right here with CineTab's controls. Click Watch together in the player to create a room: it carries on with the same video for everyone, each person watching on YouTube's own player, in sync. In a room, the Queue tab searches YouTube too. Search results come from YouTube through public Piped servers; if they're busy, search on YouTube and paste the link instead.

        Can I install CineTab as an app on my phone?

        Yes. Tap Install app at the top of this page. On Android (Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet) it installs straight away; on iPhone and iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. CineTab then opens full screen from your home screen like any app, and its page loads even on a weak connection.

        What does the dashboard remember?

        The YouTube videos and playlists you watched and where you were in them, and the names and lengths of the videos you opened from your device. It's kept only in this browser and never sent anywhere; remove one item with its ×, or everything with Clear. In Chrome and Edge on a computer, a video from your device opens again with one click (the browser asks for permission); elsewhere you pick the file again and it continues where you stopped.

        Should I use Picture-in-Picture while sharing?

        No — PiP moves the video out of the tab, so people on the call see an empty player. Use fullscreen instead.
